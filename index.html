About Us

Brewed with
Obsession

We roast in the quiet hours, chasing the perfect crack. Every bean tells a story of altitude, soil, and patience. Mad Coffee is where ritual meets rebellion.

From farm to cup

We partner directly with farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. No middlemen, no compromise—just green beans chosen by hand and roasted in small batches to unlock their true character.

Our cafe is open dawn to dusk. Come for the espresso, stay for the silence.
